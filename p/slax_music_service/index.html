Slax (Music Software)
From Fakepedia, the made up encyclopedia
Slax was a music service and music player[1] designed, developed, and maintained by SlinxWare2k4 (Later SlinxWare Software, Inc.[2]) that specialized in various forms of Electronic Music. Slax specialized in distributing music made specifically with trackers[3], but also offered other types of music[4]. Slax was first released for Windows and OS X in 2005, with a web client launching in 2007. Slax made it easy to upload music and distribute it[5] globally for free[6], though the times users had to wait for approval were known to be long[7]. SlinxWare Software was bought out by The SL Group in 2011[8], and Slax was shut down in 2013 with its library being merged and accounts migrated to SL Music[9].

Reception
Slax was generally recived positively in its first few years due to it making it easy for users to retrieve the source module files for songs. This later turned to backlash when SlinxWare Software, Inc. was formed in 2008 and removed the downloads for the source files, offering only .WAV and .MP3 downloads. This made the service more conventional, but most artists continued to use more conventional music services, causing Slax to decline in popularity signifigantly.
